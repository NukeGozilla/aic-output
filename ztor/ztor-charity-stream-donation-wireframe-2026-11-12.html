Charity stream donations: business requirements

supercatkei x Ztor · 22 or 23 Dec 2026 · updated 2026-11-20

Business requirements

Version 1.0 · 20 Nov 2026 · For Ztor biz and Denise's team · Draft for review · The wireframes in tabs 1 to 3 show each requirement on screen

1 · Background and goal

Denise (supercatkei) runs a charity stream on Twitch on 22 or 23 Dec 2026, for 6 to 10 hours. Last year donations went through a PayPal link, a script and a Google Sheet. On the night, the same payment was sent about 15 times: about 70 payments became 1,020 messages. Each donation took about 2 minutes to reach their sheet and longer to reach the bar, and the bar jumped in bursts.

This year Ztor hosts the whole donation flow: the donation page, the live bar and alerts on stream, and the records afterwards. Ztor appears as sponsor; Denise's branding leads.

What success looks like

  • Every donation counted exactly once: the total on stream matches PayPal's records to the cent.
  • A donation shows on stream within 5 seconds of payment.
  • No donation lost or delayed during bursts: tested with 100 or more donations within a few minutes.
  • No offensive name or message reaches the stream.
  • Donors can give without creating an account, in as few steps as possible.

2 · Scope

In scope

  • Donation page, co-branded, opened from Twitch chat or a QR code BR-01 to BR-08
  • PayPal payment into Denise's team's PayPal account, in SGD BR-05
  • Banned-word check on names and messages before payment BR-03
  • Live donation bar and donation alerts on stream BR-09 · BR-10
  • Admin screen for Ztor ops: campaign setup, donations list, corrections, export BR-13 to BR-16
  • Copy of each donation to Denise's Streamlabs, as a failsafe BR-12
  • The bar in Denise's team's own design BR-11

Out of scope

  • Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayNow (PayPal only for this campaign)
  • Twitch alerts for subs, raids and bits: they stay on Streamlabs
  • Tax-deductible receipts: the page says donations are not tax-deductible
  • Platform fees: Ztor takes no fee in this flow
  • Artists setting up their own campaigns
  • A page after the campaign ends: the link is taken down

4 · User stories

  • US-01: As a donor, I want to donate from the stream link without creating an account, so that giving takes under a minute.
  • US-02: As a donor, I want my name and message shown on stream, so that Denise and the community see my support.
  • US-03: As Denise's team, we want the bar and alerts to update by themselves, so that we never fix totals by hand during the stream.
  • US-04: As Denise's team, we want offensive names and messages stopped before payment, so that nothing harmful reaches the stream and no one pays for a blocked message.
  • US-05: As Ztor ops, I want to set up the campaign and correct donations myself, so that the stream does not wait on developers.
  • US-06: As Ztor ops, I want a full export of donations, so that Denise's team can show the charity and the community exactly what was raised.

5 · User flow

Main path

  1. Donor taps the Ztor link in Twitch chat or scans the QR code on stream. The donation page opens in Denise's branding. BR-01
  2. Donor picks a preset amount or types a custom one, and can add a display name and a message. They enter their email and choose whether to get news. BR-02 · BR-04
  3. Ztor checks the name and message against the campaign's banned-word list.
    🔀 A banned word is found: the word is highlighted and the donor cannot continue until they change it or remove the message.
    🔀 None found: the donor continues. BR-03
  4. Donor pays in the PayPal window. Their email is already filled in.
    🔀 The donor cancels: they return to the page with their amount kept. Nothing is recorded. BR-05
  5. PayPal confirms the payment to Ztor. BR-05
  6. Ztor records the donation once, however many times PayPal confirms it. BR-05 · BR-14
  7. Donor sees the thank-you page and gets a receipt email. BR-07
  8. Stream: the bar total moves and the alert plays the donor's name, amount and message, within 5 seconds of payment. BR-09 · BR-10
  9. Ztor sends a copy of the donation to Denise's Streamlabs (split 2). The bar and alert never wait for this copy. It is a record only: Streamlabs never plays an alert for it. BR-12

Other paths

  • S$500 with the vinyl reward on: after step 2 the donor enters a shipping address and phone number. When all copies are claimed, the reward shows as sold out and the S$500 tier can still be chosen without it. BR-06
  • Refund or chargeback in PayPal: Ztor updates the donation's status and lowers the total by itself. BR-15
  • Ztor's service is down on the night: donors can still give through a backup route that Ztor dev will propose; those donations reach the total once Ztor is back. Denise's team does nothing. BR-16

7 · Acceptance criteria

  • AC-01 (BR-01, BR-05): GIVEN a viewer with no Ztor account, WHEN they tap the link and pay S$10, THEN the donation is recorded and the money is in Denise's team's PayPal account.
  • AC-02 (BR-03): GIVEN a banned word in the name or the message, WHEN the donor tries to continue, THEN the word is highlighted and payment cannot start.
  • AC-03 (BR-03): Removing the message or changing the word lets the donor continue.
  • AC-04 (BR-05, BR-09): GIVEN PayPal confirms the same payment several times, THEN the donation is counted once and the total moves once.
  • AC-05 (BR-09, BR-10): GIVEN 100 donations within 3 minutes, THEN each shows on stream once, in order, within 5 seconds of payment.
  • AC-06 (BR-06): GIVEN one vinyl copy left and two S$500 donors paying at once, THEN only one gets the copy; the other is told it is sold out before paying.
  • AC-07 (BR-10): A donation below the alert minimum moves the total but plays no alert.
  • AC-08 (BR-14): The export lists every donation with its status, and its paid total equals the total shown on stream.
  • AC-09 (BR-15): GIVEN ops hide a message, THEN it disappears from the page, the donor list and the bar; the name and amount stay. An alert that has already played is not affected.
  • AC-09b (BR-15): GIVEN ops mark a donation voided, THEN the total drops by its amount, and no money moves in PayPal.
  • AC-10 (BR-15): A refund or chargeback in PayPal lowers the total without anyone acting in Ztor.
  • AC-11 (BR-16): GIVEN Ztor's service is down, THEN the chat link and the QR code still let a donor give, no donation is counted twice, and Denise's team takes no action.
  • AC-12 (BR-12): The bar and alerts keep working when Streamlabs is slow or down.
  • AC-13 (BR-12): GIVEN a donation is copied to Streamlabs, THEN no Streamlabs alert plays for it.

8 · Rules and edge cases

Do

  • Count a donation only when PayPal confirms it, never when the donor returns to the thank-you page.
  • Show the gross amount on stream; keep PayPal's fee and the net amount in the export.
  • Keep the amount visible on every step of the donation.

Don't

  • Don't let a backup route count the same donation twice.
  • Don't ask Denise's team to act when Ztor's service fails: covering a Ztor failure is Ztor's job.
  • Don't show a hidden or blocked message anywhere: page, list, bar or alert.
  • Don't touch Denise's Twitch alerts for subs, raids and bits.

Edge cases

  • Donor closes PayPal halfway: nothing is recorded; the page keeps the amount.
  • Payment pending in PayPal: it counts only once PayPal confirms it.
  • Two alerts at once: alerts play one after another, never on top of each other.
  • Streamlabs plays its own alert for a copy: each donor would be thanked twice. Ztor skips the Streamlabs alert on every copy; dev confirms Streamlabs honours this in week 1.
  • No target set: the overlay shows the amount raised without a goal bar.

Dev brief: Streamlabs copy

Draft for Ztor product to edit · 20 Nov 2026 · For Ztor dev · Covers BR-12, AC-12, AC-13 · Updated 20 Nov: Streamlabs is a record only, not an alert backup · Brief due Fri 27 Nov; build 7 to 11 Dec; dry run 14 to 18 Dec

1 · What it is for

Denise's team's flow, step 4: "Ztor records the donation data both on Ztor's database AND sends it to StreamLabs as a failsafe." Every paid donation is also sent to her Streamlabs account, so her team has a second record in a tool they already use. It is a record only: alerts come from Ztor alone, and if Ztor's overlay fails, Ztor covers it with its own solution (BR-16, for dev to propose), not with Streamlabs.

2 · Normal running

  1. PayPal confirms a donation as Paid.
  2. Ztor's bar and alert update straight away (BR-09, BR-10).
  3. Ztor adds a copy to a send queue: donor name, amount in SGD, message (after the banned-word check), and the Ztor donation ID.
  4. A worker sends copies to Streamlabs in order. Admin 2C shows each copy as Queued, Sent or Failed.
  5. Each copy is sent with Streamlabs' alert skipped, so Streamlabs never plays an alert.

3 · Alerts

Streamlabs never plays donation alerts. Every copy is sent with the skip_alert option set to "yes" (Streamlabs API reference, create donation: dev.streamlabs.com/reference/donations-1). Dev confirms in week 1 that this stops the alert on her stream. If Ztor's overlay fails, recovery is part of the failover dev is proposing under BR-16, not this brief.

4 · Rules to settle

#QuestionProposed answer
1Which donations are copied?Paid donations only. Voids, refunds, chargebacks and offline entries are not sent. Ztor's total is the record; admin already notes that the Streamlabs total can differ.
2How fast can copies go?Not confirmed. An earlier note quoted a Streamlabs recommendation of 2 posts a minute per user, but the current API reference gives no limit. Dev finds the real limit in week 1. Last year's peak was about 1 donation a minute.
3How far behind is acceptable?Any delay up to the end of the stream. Admin shows how many copies are waiting.
4A copy fails to sendRetry automatically a few times, then mark it Failed, with a Retry button on its row in 2C. A copy is marked Sent only after Streamlabs accepts it.
5A copy is retried after a timeoutRisk: the same donation appears twice in Streamlabs. Dev checks whether Streamlabs can recognise a repeat (for example by the Ztor donation ID). If it can't, retry only on a clear failure.
6Ops hides a messageIf the copy is still queued, it is sent without the message. If it was already sent, it stays in Streamlabs: the API reference shows no way to edit or delete a donation (dev to confirm).
7Streamlabs is down or the connection lapsesCopies wait in the queue, and the Ztor bar and alerts carry on (AC-12). Admin 2B shows the connection as lost.

5 · Setup

  • Ztor creates a free Streamlabs account and registers Ztor's app under it. Start Mon 23 Nov; Streamlabs' own approval time is unknown.
  • Denise logs in to Streamlabs once and approves Ztor's app, by Mon 7 Dec.
  • Admin 2B shows the connection and a Test write button.

6 · Dry run checks (14 to 18 Dec)

  • No alert: copies arrive in Streamlabs and no Streamlabs alert plays on her stream (AC-13).
  • Burst: 10 donations in one minute all arrive; note how long the queue takes to clear.
  • Failure: disconnect Streamlabs. Copies show Failed, the Ztor bar and alerts keep working, and Retry sends the copies once Streamlabs is reconnected.
  • Hidden message: hide one while it is queued; the copy arrives without the message.

7 · Not in scope

  • Syncing refunds, voids or hidden messages after a copy is sent
  • Reading anything back from Streamlabs
  • Streamlabs' own bar
