Charity stream donation — brief and wireframes

supercatkei x Ztor · 22 or 23 Dec 2026 · draft 2026-11-12

Brief

Draft 2026-11-12 · direction proposed by Jeff, to be confirmed with the biz team, management and dev · nothing here has been sized by a developer

1 · The ask

  • Denise (supercatkei) runs a Twitch charity stream on 22 or 23 Dec 2026, 6 to 10 hours. Last year: about S$12K through a PayPal link.
  • Biz asks: can Ztor host the donation link, with the total shown live on her stream?
  • Ztor is asked to fund 25 numbered vinyl records (about S$1.1K), given to S$500 donors.
  • Answer to Denise is due 16 to 17 Nov.

2 · Proposed direction

  • All donations on Ztor, preset amounts and custom amounts.
  • A dedicated donation flow, not the shop and not crowdfunding.
  • Built fast-lane style: lean spec, AI-assisted build, a simple Creator Studio screen without a design round, designer polish as round two.
  • Three pieces, one tab each in this document: Ztor donor page · Creator Studio for ops · stream overlay.

3 · Why a dedicated flow

RouteProblem for this campaign
CrowdfundingHidden from the site during the Antom review · needs a target, Denise has none · charges only when a target is met
Shop (eShop)Fixed prices only, no custom amount · every order becomes a pickup order with a QR and a "show this to staff" email · no shipping address · not yet used by real buyers · tied to release 2.3
Dedicated donation flowNew build, but small: one page, one ops screen, one overlay · reuses the PayPal integration already live · gives the stream total for free

4 · Round one scope

In

  • Campaign page opened straight from Twitch
  • Preset amounts and custom amount with minimum and maximum
  • S$500 limited tier: 25 units, numbered, cap enforced on the server
  • Shipping address collected at checkout for the S$500 tier
  • Optional donor name and message
  • PayPal payment, confirmed on the server
  • Donation-worded thank-you page and email
  • Ops screen: create and edit campaigns, test and live modes, donations list, void, export
  • Stream overlay page: running total, latest donations, vinyl counter

Out (round two or later)

  • Per-donation alerts with sound on stream
  • Streamlabs / StreamElements integration
  • Fee settings: platform fee is waived, payout to the charity partner is by hand
  • Creators managing their own campaigns: ops only
  • Donor tax receipts: none, and the page says so
  • Designer polish

7 · Risks

  • PayPal account (above): a business risk to accept or not; no build removes it.
  • Fixed date, new build: real money from the public on a live stream. The payment and total logic needs developer review and real small payments on production before the stream.
  • Capacity: the same developers are finishing release 2.3; one team member is on leave 19 Nov to 4 Dec.
  • Checkout friction: heavier than last year's single PayPal link if an account is required. The charity could raise less.
  • On the night: bursts of sign-ups and payments, email-code delays, the race for 25 vinyl units, and no one yet named to be on call.
