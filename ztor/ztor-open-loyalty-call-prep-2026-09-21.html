02Flow 1 — where Open Loyalty sits

Ztor keeps identity, commerce and access rules, sends events, and reads results. The loyalty logic lives in Open Loyalty: members, one wallet per artist, the ledger, scoring and tiers.

03Flow 2 — one purchase, many artists

A film rental credits every artist on the title by job weight. One NT$100 rental of a 17-credit film becomes 17 ledger entries across 17 wallets. We send one event with the artists and their weights.

04Flow 3 — from raw points to a tier, inside Open Loyalty

We want decay, ranking and tiering to run in Open Loyalty, not in a layer of ours. For each highlighted step: is it native, configurable, or on your roadmap?

05Flow 4 — how creator sites connect

Every site, including ztor.com, talks to one Ztor Creator API. Open Loyalty sits behind it and never takes calls from a site directly.
