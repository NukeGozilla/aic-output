02Flow 1 — where Open Loyalty sits

Ztor keeps identity, commerce and access rules. Open Loyalty holds members, one wallet per artist, the ledger and tiers. A thin adapter on our side does the parts that are specific to our model.

03Flow 2 — one purchase, many artists

A film rental credits every artist on the title by job weight. One NT$100 rental of a 17-credit film becomes 17 ledger entries across 17 wallets.

04Flow 3 — from raw points to a tier

A fan's score for an artist is the time-decayed sum of their ledger entries. Tiers are percentile cuts within each artist's fan base, recomputed weekly. We expect to compute both and write the tier back.

05Flow 4 — two ways to connect a creator site

Either every call goes through our API, or the creator's site reads from Open Loyalty directly while Ztor stays the only writer of points.
