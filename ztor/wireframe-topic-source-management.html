📐 Wireframe Notes — Topic Management (Updated: Core Standup April 8)
1 Week selector — discovery runs weekly (set Sunday). Navigate between weeks to review past/future topics.
2 AI Discovery → Human Curation — AI surfaces ~20 trending topics with suggested frequency. Content team approves ~10, sets frequency per topic. "Approve + Schedule" is one action.
3 Per-topic frequency (NEW) — each approved topic gets its own generation schedule: Daily, Every 2 days, Weekly, Specific dates, or One-time. Replaces the old "persistent topic" concept — frequency IS the persistence model. A topic set to "Weekly" auto-generates each week until paused.
4 Per-topic article generation — approved topics show "Generate Articles" button. Each topic can produce 1–2 articles per cycle. Article count visible on card.
5 Telegram ad-hoc in unified view (NEW) — topics triggered via Telegram (/news or /news url) also appear here with "Telegram · Ad-Hoc" badge. Shows who triggered it and when. Ad-hoc defaults to "One-time" but can be promoted to a recurring schedule (e.g., daily) if the story has legs.
6 Ad-Hoc tab — dedicated tab to filter only Telegram-triggered topics. Content team can see all ad-hoc activity at a glance.
7 Run Discovery Now — manual trigger for AI topic scan outside the weekly cycle. Useful for breaking news weeks.
8 Category filter — filter by film, music, gaming, anime, celebrity, etc. Matches BR-01 tagging.

📐 Wireframe Notes — Source Management
1 Dashboard replaces Google Sheet — all source CRUD happens here. No more spreadsheet as source of truth (confirmed with Howard, April 7).
2 Status indicators — Active (green), Paused (amber), Error (red). Error state shows failure count, aligns with BR-01 AC-01d (3+ consecutive failures → alert).
3 Crawl frequency per source — not all sources need hourly crawl. Let operators set cadence per source to manage load.
4 Category tagging — maps to BR-01 content categories (film, music, gaming, anime, celebrity, brand). Used for topic discovery and article generation.
5 URL validation on add — system validates URL is reachable before saving (BR-06 Step 2–3). "Validate & Add" button reflects this.
6 Inbox for new source requests — from Howard meeting: content team requests new sources, Howard reviews and sets up crawlers. "Add Source" is the inbox entry point.
7 Language field — critical for Ztor's TW market. Avoids simplified Chinese content on traditional Chinese site (poster issue from meeting).
