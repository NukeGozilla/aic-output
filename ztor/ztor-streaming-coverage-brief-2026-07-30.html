04Why you can trust the arithmetic為何算術可信

We re-derived the study's numbers independently from the source. They close: 我們依原始資料獨立重新推導了研究中的數字，全部吻合：

  • Media-type split → headline媒體類型拆分 → 主指標: 0.755 × 7.2% + 0.245 × 18.7% = 10.02%✓
  • Cohort split is exhaustive群組拆分無遺漏: 444,685 + 33,115 = 477,800✓
  • Cohort split → headline群組拆分 → 主指標: 0.931 × 8.8% + 0.069 × 25.4% = 9.94%✓
  • Net-growth check淨成長檢查: 8.9% × 477,800 + 5,221 ≈ 47,745 vs 47,711✓
  • Path attribution路徑歸因: 8,804 + 728 = 9,532 / 9,655✓
  • OTT cross-checkOTT 交叉驗證: 19% × 7,320 ≈ 1,390 vs 1,397✓

Segment tables usually don't survive this treatment. Practical consequence: the segment table is safe to prioritize against, even though the study also carries several stale numbers. Treat the two classes differently — don't discard the good work because of the stale figures, and don't quote the stale figures because the rest is good. 分群表通常撐不過這種檢驗。實務含意：即使研究中同時夾帶數個 stale 數字，分群表仍可安全用於排定優先順序。請分開對待這兩類 — 不要因為有舊數字就否定好的工作，也不要因為其他部分可靠就引用那些舊數字。

05Architectural read架構解讀

This section is our analysis, not the contractor's.本節為我們的分析，非外包團隊內容。

5.1 · The segment table invites a harmful conclusion分群表會誘導出有害的結論

"93.1% of the catalog at 8.8% coverage" reads like dead weight. Multiply it out: 「93.1% 的片庫只有 8.8% 覆蓋率」聽起來像是沉重包袱。但把它乘開來看：

Cohort群組 Titles片目數 Coverage覆蓋率 Covered titles已覆蓋片目
Long tail (93.1%)長尾（93.1%）444,6858.8%~39,100 — 82%
Clean (6.9%)乾淨群組（6.9%）33,11525.4%~8,400 — 18%
🚫
The low-yield cohort holds 82% of the titles we actually cover.低命中率群組，涵蓋了我們實際覆蓋片目的 82%。 It's low-yield per title and dominant in absolute terms because it's 13× bigger. Deprioritizing it by hit rate would cut the branch we're standing on. 它單片命中率低，但絕對數量佔壓倒性多數，因為它大了 13 倍。若照命中率把它降級，等於鋸掉自己坐的那根樹枝。

Consequence for contractor recommendation ② (reorder scan queue by cohort hit rate):對外包團隊建議 ②（依群組命中率重排掃描佇列）的影響： it is worth either a lot or nothing, and we can't tell which. If the nightly job completes a full pass over everything due, reordering changes only which titles are fresh first — total coverage is unchanged, exactly as the contractor states (「覆蓋率一點都不會少」). It converts to real gain only if the queue is backlogged and titles are being starved. 它要嘛價值很高、要嘛完全沒價值，而我們現在無法判斷是哪一種。如果每晚的任務都能跑完所有到期片目，重排只改變哪些片先被更新 — 總覆蓋率不變，正如外包團隊所說（「覆蓋率一點都不會少」）。它只有在佇列積壓、部分片目被餓死時才會轉化為真實收益。

📊
The missing number is scan-cycle completion rate and queue depth.缺少的關鍵數字是：掃描週期完成率與佇列深度。 It's not in the study. It decides whether ② is a real item. Measure it before building it. 研究裡沒有。它決定 ② 是不是一個真的待辦。先量測，再開發。

The defensible version of ② is tiering by volatility, not by yield: an obscure 1974 film's availability changes rarely, so re-check the tail on a 60–90 day cycle and the demand head far more often than 168h. That reallocates the same budget without starving anything. ② 站得住腳的版本是依變動性分層，而非依命中率：一部冷門的 1974 年電影，其可觀看狀態極少變動，所以長尾可以 60–90 天為一輪重查，而高需求的頭部則遠比 168 小時更頻繁地更新。這是在同樣預算下重新配置，且不會餓死任何一群。

The only half of the pipeline with unverified correctness is the half carrying all the growth — and every cycle compounds it. 管線中唯一「正確性未經驗證」的那一半，正是承載全部成長的那一半 — 而且每跑一輪就複利累積一次。

The gate is normalized-title Dice ≥ 0.82 + media-type agreement, with no release-year check. That will collide on remakes and common titles — "The Killer", "Alone", most two-character Chinese titles. A false positive is a user-facing trust bug: we tell someone a film is on LiTV, they click, it isn't there. We have no false-positive rate, per-platform or overall. 比對閘是「正規化片名 Dice ≥ 0.82 + 媒體類型一致」，沒有上映年份檢查。這在重拍片與常見片名上一定會撞車 —《The Killer》、《Alone》、大多數兩個字的中文片名。誤判是直接面向使用者的信任問題：我們告訴使用者某片在 LiTV，他點進去，卻沒有。我們沒有任何誤判率數據，不論是各平台別或整體。

🛠
yh_vod mirrors TMDB, so release_date is already present. A year-proximity gate is nearly free to add. At minimum, log the year delta on every match so this becomes auditable retroactively. yh_vod 是 TMDB 的鏡像，release_date 早就存在。加一道年份接近度檢查幾乎是零成本。至少也該在每次比對時記錄年份差，讓這件事日後可回溯稽核。

06Change the metric first先改指標

Full-catalog coverage % measures us against TMDB's global archive. That archive is not our product. 全庫覆蓋率是拿我們跟 TMDB 的全球典藏比。那個典藏不是我們的產品。

Nobody at Ztor cares whether we have watch links for 477,800 titles including every unrated 1960s regional film that TMDB happens to list. What matters is whether a user searching ztor.com finds a watch link. Ztor 沒有人在乎我們是否對 477,800 部片（包含 TMDB 剛好收錄的每一部無評分 1960 年代地方電影）都有觀看連結。真正重要的是：在 ztor.com 上搜尋的使用者，能不能找到觀看連結。

This changes decisions, not just reporting:這改變的是決策，不只是報表：

  • It answers whether users are hitting these gaps at all它回答了使用者究竟有沒有撞上這些缺口
