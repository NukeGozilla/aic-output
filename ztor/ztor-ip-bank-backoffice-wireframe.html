Payout methods — v4, biz-confirmed 2026-07-22
① Flat fee 一次性費用 — one-time fixed amount, independent of revenue performance. No cap.
② Revenue share + Minimum guarantee — net revenue × share %, share capped at 30% of net. At the 1-year mark, if the accrued share is below the MG the system auto-pays the MG; above it, tiered calculation continues. MG is non-refundable and not an advance — never deducted from future share.
③ Net revenue share — straight net revenue × share %, capped at 50% of net. No minimum guarantee.
Flat fee and MG amounts carry their own currency (NT$ / US$ / HK$ / S$); revenue accrues in NT$, so cross-currency comparison and payout convert at the settlement-day FX rate.
⚠️ Pending biz before implementation: final net-revenue formula (Tastemaker commission in or out) · Type 2 tier table beyond the MG · settlement processing time (real-time or batched) · FX rate source for non-NT$ amounts.
↩

Owed is derived per payout method: net-share and MG rows accrue net revenue × effective share % (Type 2 clips at 30% of net, Type 3 at 50% — the excess stays with the platform); flat-fee rows are a one-time amount paid from the platform remainder. Withdrawals reuse the existing Profile withdraw flow (not configured here). Pending owners accrue but cannot withdraw until they claim. Ztor has no universal settlement period — withdrawal is the only event that makes money final, so a backdated entry genuinely recalculates every un-withdrawn balance and posts a reasoned transaction line on each side (ADR 2026-08-04). Revenue is attributed by date, so a mid-month start resolves exactly. “Net revenue” definition and the settlement/calculation engine are out of scope for this phase — figures shown are illustrative.
