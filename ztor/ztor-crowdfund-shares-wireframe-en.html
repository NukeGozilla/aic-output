Why these changes

  1. The original form's flaw: price/share = goal ÷ total shares, but non-share (donate) tiers also count toward the goal → the derived price was fiction (where this whole discussion started)
  2. One number, one job: goal = funding threshold (all tiers count); valuation = pricing base → donations can never move the issue price
  3. Model B fixed per-share: each share's % is known at publish, no settlement step — backers understand it in one sentence
  4. Disclosable issue price (IPO concept): tiers price at a premium (perks in) or discount (creator's offer) freely; paves the way for future share resale
