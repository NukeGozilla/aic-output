Context & Why This Exists

Decision Framing

Deck v1.2 draws six services behind the facade; Loyalty is one of them, and every capability that depends on it — Fan Club, Points, Ranking, Rewards, Missions — is still phase TBD. The deck's own de-scope proposal is:

💡 Deck v1.2 De-scope Proposal

"A fan club does not require the loyalty engine." Binary membership can ship first; tiering and point-burn can follow.

So the question on the table is whether we build that engine, buy it, or defer it past the 10/31 LLOUD launch. This research is the buy-side homework: what Open Loyalty actually offers, what it costs, and where it does and doesn't fit what we already locked.

3 Commercial & Pricing Model

Quote-Based Only

No credible public MSRP exists. Open Loyalty pricing is strictly custom and negotiated via sales:

💳 Published Billing Structure

Two-Part Tariff: Platform Fee (fixed annual/monthly base) + Allowance Fee (variable, scaled on monthly Active Members).

Definition of Active Member: A registered member with at least one loyalty event in that calendar month (source).

Community & Industry Consensus

  • Trophy comparison: Confirms quote-based enterprise pricing with sales demo required.
  • Opia Review: Lists pricing as "Contact Open Loyalty for custom quote".
  • Voucherify Comparison: Notes pricing is "Custom — SaaS or on-premise deployment".
  • On the "Free Community Edition" claim: Some legacy martech blogs (e.g. AboutMartech) cite a self-hosted community edition with no license fee. Open Loyalty's live pricing page explicitly states the modern platform is pure SaaS and closed source. Treat all CE claims as outdated.
⚠️ Crucial Negotiation Lever for Fan Clubs

Artist fan clubs have a massive long tail of dormant or low-activity fans. If "active member" triggers on passive events (e.g. weekly tier recalculation, login, automated email trigger), we will pay an enterprise bill for dead accounts. We must establish in writing what specific events qualify an account as "Active".

4 Fit Against What We Already Locked

Core Semantic Divergence

Our points model (ztor-loyalty-points-model-2026-08-14, locked with L on 08-13/14) is not a generic e-commerce loyalty system. The critical areas requiring vendor confirmation:

Our Locked Requirement Why Default SaaS Engines Fail It Call Checklist & Test
Per-Creator Programs
Same fan is Inner Circle for Artist A, but non-member for Artist B 		Most engines assume 1 Brand = 1 Program. Supporting hundreds of creators inside one platform often triggers per-tenant licensing multiplier. Can one installation host N creator programs under one parent commercial contract without multiplying allowance fees?
Percentile Tiers
Top 1% (Tier 1), Top 10% (Tier 2), Top 30% (Tier 3), Rest (Base) 		Standard engines use fixed point thresholds (e.g. >1,000 pts = Gold). Percentile tiers require relative population sorting & dynamic cutoffs. Can tier placement be assigned via external rank/webhook, or is the engine strictly limited to static numeric thresholds?
45-Day Half-Life Decay
Continuous time-weighted decay over rolling 90-day window 		Expiry ≠ Decay. Standard engines support cliff expiration (e.g. points expire after 12 months). Continuous exponential decay is rare off-the-shelf. Does the engine natively support mathematical time-decay on ledger balances, or only discrete point expiration dates?
Weekly Recompute + 14d Grace
Scheduled batch re-sort with asymmetric promotion/demotion grace 		Requires custom scheduled cron logic with stateful grace counters for demotions. Is this workflow configurable within their rule engine, or must Ztor cron jobs push tier updates via API?
Auditable Reversible Ledger
Every single point traces to an immutable external event ID 		Usually supported, but idempotency guarantees and transaction rollback ergonomics vary. Confirm idempotency key handling, audit-log export fidelity, and automated reversal hooks for refunded orders.
Waterfall Spend Scoring
Different purchase categories (PPV, Merch, Tickets) yield different points 		Needs flexible multiplier rules tied to SKU metadata and product categories. Confirm SKU/category payload parsing in their campaign multiplier engine.
🎯 Synthesis: What Are We Actually Buying?

Their feature set matches our surface needs (points, wallet, rewards catalogue, admin UI, webhooks). But because our scoring semantics (percentile rank, 45-day decay) are non-standard, an off-the-shelf engine will almost certainly force us to compute the score ourselves and push it in.

If so, we are buying a Ledger + Rewards Catalogue + Admin UI, not a Scoring Engine. That may still save months of frontend and admin tooling, but it fundamentally re-indexes the price we should be willing to pay.

Architectural Boundary (Holds Regardless of Vendor Selection)

Open Loyalty would strictly operate as a leaf microservice behind the facade. All authoritative business policy remains with Ztor:

🔒 Owned by Ztor (Non-Negotiable) Core SOT
  • Identity & Auth: Member accounts, SSO, sessions
  • Creator & Channel Rules: Channel boundaries, artist settings
  • Commerce & Checkout: Orders, fiat gateway, refunds, splits
  • Vault & Entitlements: Media vault access, ticket tokens
  • Policy Service: Authoritative gatekeeper (e.g. "May this user view this right now?")
⚙️ Delegated to Open Loyalty (Vendor) Leaf Logic
  • Ledger Accounting: Member point balance, transaction records
  • Rewards Catalogue: Reward tiers, digital coupons, redemption claims
  • Admin Campaign UI: Marketer point promotions & bonus events
  • Engagement Missions: Task completion tracking & badges
  • Event Log Ingest: Raw ingest of purchase and activity webhooks

5 Vendor Call Checklist

Take to Meeting Verbatim

Interactive checklist to evaluate Open Loyalty on technical integration and commercial terms:

🛠️ Technical Access & Integration
Provide developer sandbox access with full admin privileges
Review official OpenAPI / Swagger spec or Postman collection
Verify sample payloads: purchase accrual, custom engagement event, tier upgrade, refund reversal
Review webhook retry policy, signature verification, and delivery latency SLA
💰 Commercial & Licensing
Formal quote against estimated monthly active members (obtain exact written definition of "Active")
Multi-creator model: clarify how many separate creator programs can run under one parent tier
Documented SLA terms backing the 99.99% uptime claim and P1 incident escalation paths
Total onboarding & implementation services fee (vendor engineering vs our integration time)
🎯 Algorithmic Fit (Section 4 Table)
Can tier qualification be pushed from an external ranking worker instead of static thresholds?
Does the ledger calculate half-life time-decay, or will we have to calculate decay balances ourselves?
Can transaction idempotency keys be bound directly to external Ztor order and event IDs?

6 Open Questions for Us (Not the Vendor)

Internal Strategic Forks

Before signing any vendor contract or scheduling extended technical reviews, these internal forks must be resolved:

FORK 01
Does the 10/31 LLOUD launch need a loyalty engine at all?
Deck v1.2 already suggests that Fan Club can launch as simple binary membership (paid/free subscriber) without dynamic points or tiers. If so, the entire vendor evaluation can be deferred past 10/31 without blocking launch, saving critical timeline and focus.
FORK 02
Who owns the Loyalty workstream?
The Solution Architect review flagged that none of the six white-label workstreams currently has an assigned tech or product owner. Evaluating a vendor without an owner results in pricing quotes that languish with no implementation plan.
FORK 03
Build vs. buy has not been costed on the build side.
We already have a mathematically locked model (ztor-loyalty-points-model-2026-08-14.md), and the score is purely used to sort fans within an artist. That is a narrow, well-bounded ledger and batch job. A 1-page engineering estimate for building the ledger in-house is required so any vendor quote can be measured against a real internal baseline.
