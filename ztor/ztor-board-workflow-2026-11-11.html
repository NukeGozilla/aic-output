Business decisions — on Master

On any board, set Status to Management Decision. The ticket appears on Master · To discuss for the next biz call. The decision is written as a comment on the ticket.

1To discuss
→
2Decision pending
→
3Confirmed
A confirmed card leaves Master after 14 days. The ticket stays on its product board and release.

Bugs

Plain bugs skip Master. A bug is added to Master · Inbox only when it needs a call: "bug or by design?", a fix that changes behaviour, scope or a release date, or a "bug" that is really a missing feature. It stays on the bug board too.

Weekly rhythm

Any time
Drop ideas in Master · Inbox. Ops submits the request form. QA files bugs on the bug boards.
Weekly · product grooming
Open Master. Empty the Inbox and Change & Feature Requests · Items to discuss. Each card leaves by one exit.
Weekly · biz and ops calls
Walk To discuss and Decision pending on Master. Write each decision as a comment and move the card to Confirmed.

Day-to-day status of work in build is read from the Roadmap and the product boards, not from Master.

Retired

No longer used

每週 grooming — Inbox 每張卡片都從一個出口離開

一般 bug 不經過 Master。只有需要討論時才加到 Master · Inbox:「是 bug 還是設計如此?」、修復會改變行為、範圍或發版日期,或其實是缺少的功能。它同時仍留在 bug 看板。
