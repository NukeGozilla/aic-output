What Bookyay Sends

The governing rule: "we are keeping all the rules from bookyay, we are not trying to re-invent the wheel. bookyay will send over the data. ztor to capture it. the only differences in ztor is just Ui treatment or currency."
Prices travel one way, in HKD only; other currencies are converted and fine-tuned by Ztor, and Bookyay never holds, converts or validates a non-HKD price. Field names and structures stay Bookyay's — nothing renamed, nothing re-wrapped (Q13).

⚠ Currency matters in exactly one place: the confirm call
When Ztor calls Bookyay to create the order it sends Bookyay's own HKD ticket price. What the fan actually paid, and in which currency, stays on Ztor and is viewed through the widget on the order page — Bookyay does not store it.
Everything else is HKD, one direction — prices, caps, discounts, early bird, reports. Other currencies are set on the Ztor side and Bookyay neither converts nor checks them BO-6 · API-5
