LLOUD on Ztor: what fans see, who runs it, when it moves

Component by component, instead of “Option A vs Option B”. Fans can’t tell an API layer from a platform; they see whichever features exist on launch day. So each piece of the journey is marked Ztor or LLOUD, with the date it moves onto Ztor if it starts on LLOUD’s side. Approach as of Oct 6: identity and commerce on Ztor’s Creator API; social login, loyalty + VIP and delivery integrated directly by LLOUD; payment under review; the platform layer on Ztor later, then migrate.

ZTOR runs on Ztor (Creator API, eShop, accounts) LLOUD runs on LLOUD’s side (site, own apps, own tools) → ZTOR LATER starts on LLOUD, moves onto Ztor OPEN payment option under review
Rule of thumb: anything touching money, orders or the account stays on Ztor. Presentation, social-login buttons and outbound marketing can be LLOUD’s. Dates are the PM’s planning estimates; the dev team hasn’t sized them.

4 · What it takes

From LLOUD

  • A business entity for the Google / Apple / Facebook / LINE developer accounts
  • LLOUD’s team owns the front end: the site, social-login buttons, loyalty display and VIP gating, the delivery form; and configures the loyalty vendor. Plus a Stripe business account if option 2
  • Answers: launch date and first drop · same account on ztor.com OK? · points per brand or per creator · who builds lloud.club

Where moving onto Ztor later costs something

  • Social login: provider IDs are tied to the app that registered them
  • Delivery: addresses collected on LLOUD’s side stay there
  • Loyalty: the points ledger is imported by Ztor account id; agree the points rule (per brand or per creator) before it fills
  • Everything else starts on Ztor or never moves

Open on Ztor’s side

  • Payment option 2 to be sized; under option 1 the launch runs on whichever gateway is live
  • A loyalty-vendor integration (members + order events) with a read proxy in the Creator API, and a token-exchange endpoint for LLOUD’s social apps
  • The sign-in API’s date (sprint started Oct 1)
  • The loyalty vendor is not chosen yet; Ztor’s own engine comes with the platform layer, and LLOUD’s rules become its spec
