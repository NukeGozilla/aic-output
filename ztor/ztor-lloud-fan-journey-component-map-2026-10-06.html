LLOUD on Ztor: what fans see, who runs it, when it moves

Component by component, instead of “Option A vs Option B”. Fans can’t tell an API layer from a platform; they see whichever features exist on launch day. So each piece of the journey is marked Ztor or LLOUD, with the date it moves onto Ztor if it starts on LLOUD’s side.

ZTOR runs on Ztor (Creator API, eShop, accounts) LLOUD runs on LLOUD’s side (site, own apps, own tools) → ZTOR LATER starts on LLOUD, moves onto Ztor ASSUMED loyalty has no concrete plan yet
Rule of thumb: anything touching money, orders or the account stays on Ztor. Presentation, social-login buttons and outbound marketing can be LLOUD’s. Dates are the PM’s planning estimates; the dev team hasn’t sized them.

4 · What it takes

From LLOUD

  • A business entity for the Google / Apple / Facebook / LINE developer accounts
  • LLOUD’s team owns the social-login front end and delivery operations
  • Answers: launch date and first drop · same account on ztor.com OK? · points per brand or per creator · who builds lloud.club

Where moving onto Ztor later costs something

  • Social login: provider IDs are tied to the app that registered them
  • Delivery: addresses collected on LLOUD’s side stay there
  • Loyalty: a points ledger built outside Ztor has to be imported
  • Everything else starts on Ztor or never moves

Open on Ztor’s side

  • Payment gateway is in flux; the launch runs on whichever gateway is live
  • The sign-in API’s date (sprint started Oct 1)
  • Loyalty: engine, owner and rules not decided; all loyalty dates are assumptions
