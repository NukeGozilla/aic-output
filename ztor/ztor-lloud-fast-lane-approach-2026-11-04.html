The fast lane: how LLOUD features get built, and how they reach Ztor

A LLOUD feature is specced in one page, designed, built on the Creator API and shipped on lloud.club first. The business team then sees it working and decides how it folds into ztor.com.

Draft for discussion 4 Nov 2026 Owner: Product For: L · dev · design · product · biz team
01 · In one line

LLOUD is Ztor's fast lane. Product → design → build → live on LLOUD → shown to the business team → they decide: fold into Ztor, keep it LLOUD-only, or drop it.

05 · Fold-in

How a LLOUD feature reaches ztor.com

The biz team sees three things
  • The feature working on lloud.club, and what LLOUD learned from it
  • What Ztor would need: Creator Studio screens, ztor.com front end, any policy question
  • A recommendation from product: adopt as is, adapt, LLOUD-only, or drop
Back office folds too
  • Today the LLOUD AI console holds the settings for fast-lane features (brand, sign-in, missions, vault, points rules)
  • As a feature folds into Ztor, its settings move into Creator Studio, the back office Ztor actually uses
  • End state: the console keeps partner set-up only; everything creator-facing lives in Creator Studio
Fold into ZtorSame task moves onto the Feature Board and the Roadmap release. Ztor-stream work from here.
LLOUD-onlyStays a channel feature. Revisit when a second artist site wants it.
DropRemoved from LLOUD or left dormant.
06 · In the lane now

Membership and auction

Both come from L's spec "LLOUD membership & auction mechanics" (3 Nov). Neither is in Ztor's current loyalty MVP, so they run in the fast lane first.

Membership card · LLOUD Club
1 Fan→2 Ranked Fan→3 Front Row · the card→4 Inner Circle
  • Front Row = join LLOUD Club (paid), or win a competition, or reach the points bar
  • Inner Circle = Club member and the points bar; at most 1% of fans; money alone never gets there
  • Upgrades instant; every downgrade waits 14 days with a notice
  • Points shown as dB on LLOUD, Ztor loyalty points underneath
Paid membership is a money rule: product decides whether it runs in the lane or goes through the Ztor stream.
Auction · Front Row and above
  • Lot announced at least 7 days ahead; opening price set per lot in Creator Studio
  • Round 1: 3-day public round, soft close, raise ladder
  • Cut: top 10 by each fan's own highest bid
  • Round 2: 15-minute sealed round; fans see only their own bid
  • Tie → tiebreak rounds until one highest; no sealed bids → random draw
  • Winner pays; a later level drop never blocks payment
Still open in L's spec: payment deadline and non-payment, a public round with zero bids, the membership price, the sealed-round floor.

Next in: social login providers on the hosted sign-in, members-only pieces, presale window. The December and January drops carry what is ready.
