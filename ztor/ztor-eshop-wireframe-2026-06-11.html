Decision D1 · proposed Creator = entity, not role
  • one primitive for eShop + Sept crowdfund merge
  • avoids role sprawl per feature
Decision D2 · narrowed Checkout stays with commerce module
  • Stripe + Antom · on-page, API-only
  • still open: Ztor platform cut + waterfall mapping
Decision D3 · resolved Wrap commerce module as microservice
  • multi-tenant by design · ZTor a named tenant from day one
  • Jun 11 Core meeting · Aug 12 release wave
Decision D4 · resolved Physical merch + bundled digital
  • standalone digital out of MVP scope
  • shipping: Beamco-managed or self-managed · refunds Stripe API only
Decision D5 · open Artist admin surface
  • keep Beamco star-web portal vs fold into Ztor BO
  • affects onboarding + product mgmt flow
Schedule Aug 12 release wave
  • Creator Studio swimlane · eShop ships first
  • no BRD or task breakdown yet
  • 2.0 roadmap shift pending — eShop unaffected
