CancelSave prices
  • Lowest allowed = base × rate when the price was set × (1 + swap fee). Ops can round up but can't save below it.
  • Swap fee = the payment provider's conversion charge (assumed 1%) plus a safety cushion. Set by admin per currency pair (editable here); creators see it locked.
  • Break-even rate = the rate at which this price stops covering the base after the provider's charge. It's saved with the price.
  • Alerts: Warning at half the room used · Act now at break-even, then auto-reprice after X hours (to decide). Only the fan's currency getting weaker is urgent.
