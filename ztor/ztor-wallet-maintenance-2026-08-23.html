My Wallet

🛠️

Wallet temporarily under maintenance

We're upgrading our settlement system to make sure your balance and withdrawal figures are accurate. Your earnings are safe and unaffected.

Withdrawals are paused during maintenance.

Need help with a payout in the meantime? Contact us and we'll handle it manually.

Contact support

Scope

  • Only the My Wallet route is replaced — sidebar, Sales, Events, QR all untouched
  • Wallet card + Reports section both hidden (no stale numbers anywhere on the page)
  • Copy avoids "wrong numbers" — frames it as a settlement-system upgrade

Implementation

  • One feature flag (e.g. wallet_maintenance) checked at the route level
  • Flag ON → render this card instead of wallet content
  • Also guard the withdraw API server-side — UI hiding alone doesn't stop a direct call
  • Flip flag OFF when reconciliation is done — no redeploy needed

Watch-outs

  • Check Home dashboard for any revenue/balance widgets — mask those too if present
  • Pending withdrawal requests: decide whether to process or hold (ops call)
  • Have the support path ready before flipping the flag — creators will ask where their money is
