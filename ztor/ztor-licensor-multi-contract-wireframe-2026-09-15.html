規則 The rule — one line, read the same way by dev and content

Everything below is a consequence of this one sentence.

一部影片，在同一地區，同一時間，只能屬於一份啟用中的合約。A title can sit in only one enabled contract per territory at a time. Uniqueness = 發行公司 × 影片 × 地區 + overlapping 合約日期.
✅ 不同影片 → 永遠不互擋New title → new contract, its own dates and 分潤比例. This is Quin's case: yes, per title.
✅ 同影片 · 不同地區 → 可以《新片 A》 HK contract and TW contract side by side.
✅ 同影片 · 同地區 · 日期不重疊 → 可以Renewal entered before the current one ends.
❌ 同影片 · 同地區 · 日期重疊 → 擋Error names the title and the conflicting contract.
❌ 全世界 對該影片雙向排他Title under an enabled 全世界 contract → no 香港/台灣 contract for that title. Reverse also blocked.
Changed from v1 (09-03): uniqueness key was 發行公司 × 地區 → now 發行公司 × 影片 × 地區. Under v1, the existing 全世界 contract for 《F》 (2026-08 → 2031-08) would have blocked every other contract on the account until 2031. Unchanged: multi-contract · edit after submit · audit trail · no auto-takedown at expiry.

可否建立？ Decision matrix + Quin's real case + why the rule exists

Given an existing enabled contract on the licensor, can the new (or edited) contract be saved?

#New contract contains…地區 vs existing合約日期Result
1a different titleany — same, other, or 全世界any✅ 可以
2the same titledifferent territory (香港 vs 台灣)overlap✅ 可以
3the same titlesame territoryno overlap (renewal)✅ 可以
4the same titlesame territoryoverlap❌ 擋 — names the conflict
5the same titleexisting 全世界 → new 香港 / 台灣overlap❌ 擋 — 全世界 covers it
6the same titleexisting 香港 / 台灣 → new 全世界overlap❌ 擋 — would double-cover
7the same titleexisting contract is 禁用 or 已到期any✅ 可以 — not enabled / not overlapping
Overlap test = new.start ≤ existing.end AND new.end ≥ existing.start (inclusive). Never "does another contract exist".

開發前待確認 Open before development · Not in scope

Open
  1. 地區 list — 香港 · 台灣 · 全世界 confirmed. Anything else for v1 (新加坡, given SGD is a settlement currency)? No region groups in between.
  2. Legacy contracts — existing ones already carry a 地區 (often 全世界 by default). Content team to review legacy 全世界 contracts whose titles are really territory-limited. Under per-title this no longer blocks new contracts for other titles.
  3. Amended 分潤比例 — forward-only or retroactive? (see section 5)
  4. No matching contract — title licensed for 香港 only, 台灣 viewer pays: unattributed, or fall back? Report-side.
  5. Royalty report grouping — 地區 becomes the natural group once a licensor has several contracts; decide before that report ships.
  6. Expiry auto-detect (Eric) — does a ~1-month-before-takedown routine exist today? Keep or disable, so there is one takedown path.
  7. 類型 單影片 / 多影片 — keep, or drop now that any contract holds 1..n titles?
Not in scope
  1. Contract expiry does not take content down — takedown runs off the takedown date entered at upload (confirmed 08-31). No expiry check.
  2. Contract 地區 does not geo-block playback — it is a royalty-attribution dimension. confirm
  3. Bulk import / export of contracts.
  4. Licensor-facing view of contracts in the Film Owner Portal.
