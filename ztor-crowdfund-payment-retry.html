🔁 Two-stage recovery — Step 3 retries in the background with no backer notification. If all retries fail, Step 4 hands control to the backer with an email + a recovery page that captures a new card and confirms payment on-session.

This flow picks up after the initial off-session capture has failed (a "card declined / insufficient funds / authentication required" response from Stripe at campaign deadline). The goal is two-fold: minimise backer friction by silently retrying first, then give the backer one clear path to fix it themselves before the pledge is forfeited.

3 Background smart retries

After the first charge fails, the background retry mechanism kicks in — either Stripe Smart Retries or a custom cron job, whichever the team adopts. These retries run silently: the backer is not notified.

4 Manual user recovery

After all auto-retries fail, control passes back to the backer. The goal is to get them onto the site, enter a new card, and confirm payment immediately while still on-session.

⏰ Recovery window: 7 days. Starting from when the recovery email is sent, the backer has 7 days to complete payment on a new card. Anything past that → the pledge is forfeited: the order is voided, no rewards ship, and the tier slot is released.

Key implementation details

  • Use PaymentIntent, not SetupIntent — this stage charges and saves the card in one step, not two.
  • Pass setup_future_usage="off_session" on the PaymentIntent so the new card is retained for future top-ups or renewals.
  • Confirm the payment on-session. Don't queue it for a background worker — the backer is right there in the browser, and 3DS authentication only works while they're present.

✕ Payment fails

If 7-day deadline expires without success → pledge is forfeited: order voided, no rewards ship, tier slot released.

🎯 Why this two-stage shape

Silent retries minimise friction
Most card-decline causes (temporary insufficient funds, network blip, issuer rate-limit) clear within days without the backer doing anything. Notifying them on every retry is noisy and erodes trust. Stage 3 absorbs that.
On-session for the manual step
Off-session charges can't complete 3D Secure flows. Forcing on-session confirmation in Stage 4 means SCA-required cards (most of EU/UK + many APAC issuers) can actually finish the payment.
PaymentIntent over SetupIntent
SetupIntent only saves a card; we'd still need a second hop to charge it. PaymentIntent + setup_future_usage does both in one request, with one 3DS challenge, in one user gesture.
7-day deadline is firm
Beyond 7 days, the project may have moved into production and capacity planning depends on a final backer count. Forfeiting late-paying pledges keeps the production timeline honest.
