Payment Recovery Flow Biz overview

When a backer's pledge charge fails — automated retries first, then a clear path for the backer to fix it before the pledge expires.

Lifecycle Pledge happy path State machine System architecture Payment recovery Live prototype ↗
🔁 Two phases, one flow — the system tries to fix the failure on its own first (silently, without bothering the backer). Only if that fails do we email the backer and ask them to update their card.

This flow picks up after a pledge's first charge attempt has failed at the campaign deadline (card declined, expired, etc.). The goal is to recover the pledge with as little backer friction as possible, while still giving the project a firm cut-off so production planning isn't held hostage by unresolved payments.

⏰ 7-day recovery window. Starting from when the recovery email is sent, the backer has 7 days to complete payment on a new card. If they don't, the pledge is automatically forfeited — the order is voided, no rewards ship, and the tier slot is released so another backer can take it.

✓ Recovered successfully

  • Pledge flips to confirmed
  • Backer receives confirmation email
  • Rewards ship per the project's fulfillment schedule
  • If silent retries did the job, backer never even knew there was a problem

✗ Forfeited (7 days expired)

  • Pledge is voided — no charge, no rewards
  • Tier slot is released back into inventory
  • Backer receives a final notification email
  • Creator's funded total is adjusted in the dashboard

📅 Day-by-day from a backer's point of view

Day 0
Campaign deadline. Stripe attempts to charge each backer's saved card. Most succeed; this flow only kicks in for the ones that don't.
Day 0–7
Silent auto-retries. The system tries 3 more times spread over the week. Backer is not notified — most card declines are temporary and resolve on their own.
Day 7
If still failing → recovery email goes out. Backer clicks through to a page where they enter a new card and confirm the charge there and then.
Day 7–14
7-day window to act. Backer can update their card and retry as many times as needed. Every attempt is on-the-spot — they see success or failure immediately.
Day 14
Forfeit deadline. If no successful payment by now, the pledge is voided and the tier slot is released.
