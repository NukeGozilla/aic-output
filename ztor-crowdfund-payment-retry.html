🔁 Three recovery checkpoints — we don't give up after the first failed charge. The system retries silently for 7 days, then asks the backer to update their card for another 7 days, then escalates to ops only if both windows expire.

Crowdfund runs all-or-nothing: backers are not charged when they click support — we only record their card. At campaign close, if the goal is met, we charge every backer in batch. This diagram covers that batch charge and what happens when some payments fail.

Three recovery checkpoints

Each checkpoint gives the failing payment another chance to clear before we escalate.

① · 7 days

Auto-retry

The system retries the charge silently in the background — backer is not notified. Most temporary declines (insufficient funds, network blip) clear within this window on their own.

② · 7 days

Manual recovery

If auto-retries fail, the backer gets an email with a link to update their card. They have 7 days to enter a new card and complete the payment in one on-the-spot session.

③ · Manual

Ops cancel + refund

If both windows expire and the gap is material, ops reviews the situation and may cancel the project — refunding everyone who did pay. Manual decision, not automatic.

📅 Worst-case timeline from a backer's point of view

Day 0 → 30
Campaign live. Backer clicks support, card info is recorded — no charge yet.
Day 30
Campaign closes. If goal hit, every backer is charged in batch. Most charges succeed cleanly; the rest enter recovery.
Day 30 → 37
① 7-day auto-retry. Silent background retries. Backer doesn't know anything is happening; project payout to creator is held until this window settles.
Day 37
Recovery email sent to any backers whose charges are still failing. Page link lets them enter a new card and pay on the spot.
Day 37 → 44
② 7-day manual recovery. Backer can update their card and retry as many times as needed within the window.
Day 44+
③ Ops decision. If the failed-payment gap is material, ops considers cancelling the project and refunding everyone who already paid. Manual call, not automatic.
