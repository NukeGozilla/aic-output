🔒 Stripe Future Payment — Step 4 collects card details only. Charge happens at Phase 3 of the lifecycle (campaign deadline), not now.

The happy path assumes an eligible logged-in backer (has joined the linked Creator’s community → holds 1 LP on that creator) on a Live campaign. Edge cases — ineligibility, card decline at capture, project canceled mid-flight — are covered in the lifecycle flowchart. Tier price displays in the creator's currency (HKD/TWD); other regions see live FX preview marked "參考價格".

📅 What the backer sees after step 5

Immediately
Confirmation screen — pledge ID shown. Email N1 fires. CTA "查看我的贊助" goes to /profile/hungie?tab=pledges.
Anytime before campaign deadline
Profile pledges tab shows the pledge with status pill 待扣款. Backer can revisit project page; cannot cancel from UI in Stage 2.
Campaign deadline (success)
Stripe batch capture fires in creator currency at Stripe daily FX rate. Status flips to 已扣款. Email N5 sent. Profile updates. If project collects shipping, ops asks backer to confirm address.
Campaign deadline (failure)
No charge made. Status stays 待扣款 momentarily then transitions per project state. Email N7 sent.
Project Released (success path)
Redeem code issued — unique, one-time. Backer activates within 30 days; once activated, has 48 hours to finish. No POPCORN charge.
Physical perks (if applicable)
Project's collectsAddress flag determines whether shipping is collected. Ops handles fulfillment manually (順豐 / similar) and bulk-imports tracking.

🎯 Why this flow shape

Eligibility BEFORE terms
Don't let an ineligible user read terms only to be blocked at the end. Step 2 short-circuits with a clear "加入社群即可贊助" CTA.
Terms BEFORE card
Legal needs explicit, time-stamped acceptance of the 製作支持-not-投資 framing before any payment instrument is captured.
Trust pill above tiers (detail page)
"Stripe Future Payment · 募資成功才扣款" appears above the tier grid so users know the answer to "will I be charged now?" before they click.
Profile-tab my-pledges
Pledges live on the user profile (not a standalone page) so they sit alongside posts/likes/saves — matches the rest of Ztor's UX pattern.
