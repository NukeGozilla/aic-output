v5.3 update (2026-05-19) — Funding outcome split into two states per Asana 1214919…5433 (section 1.17 Crowdfunding - Simple version (May 31st)).

· 目標達成 (Goal Achieved) — system trigger. Pledged total met goal at Day 30; batch capture begins; retry sequence may run (see payment retry).
· 募資成功 (Successfully Funded) — ops trigger. NEW. All backer payments collected (retry concluded); funds in hand, ready for disbursement.

Why split: Today's diagram jumped from FUNDED → IN PRODUCTION in one hop, papering over the 7–14+ day retry window. Splitting gives ops a clean handoff state once collections are confirmed and unblocks the disbursement step from any in-flight retry uncertainty.
