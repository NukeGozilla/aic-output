§7 — GUARDRAILS

Do · Don't · Edge cases

✓ DO

  • Lead with fiat pricing in creator currency (SGD or USD)
  • Log every state transition with operator + timestamp
  • Default eligibility CTA: "加入社群即可贊助"
  • Show share allocation transparently in plan cards
  • Tell creators: payout up to 30 days from close
  • Mandate Notify-Users on milestone date change
  • Treat shares-to-cash as the unified reward model

✗ DON'T

  • Price shares individually — they come from plans
  • Reintroduce 追蹤 / follow verbs
  • Gate eligibility on Popcorn balance
  • Allow fan-side cancel during funding
  • Build in-admin refund UI (Stripe back-office only)
  • Allow downgrade or self-cancel of a pledge
  • Allow multiple active pledges per fan per campaign

▲ EDGE CASES

  • LP service down → treat as ineligible; soft retry
  • User unjoins after pledging → pledge valid, LP persists
  • Webhook delay > 1h → admin surfaces stale-pending
  • Cross-region decimals → primary currency wins
  • Last-spot race → first-write-wins · loser → waitlist
  • No Stripe Connect → manual Finance bank transfer
  • Music flow change mid-campaign → freeze source
