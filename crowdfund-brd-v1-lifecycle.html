Worst-case timeline · backer POV

Day 0 → 30
Campaign live. Backer pledges; card locked, no charge. Max 30-day window. Auto-flip to Goal Achieved the moment all shares sell out.
Day 30
Campaign closes. Status check on end date: sharesSold == total → already Goal Achieved · sharesSold ≥ min → Minimum Met · sharesSold < min → Unsuccessful. Batch charge runs for Goal Achieved + Minimum Met.
Day 30 → 40
Recovery on declines. 3-day silent retry + 7-day email recovery URL. Day-10 fail → FIFO waitlist swap (BR-31, one-for-one share replacement) → re-run 3+7 cycle on the replacement.
Day 40 → 50
Waitlister recovery (worst case). Second 3+7 cycle if the substitute also declines. Loop continues until queue exhausted.
Day 50
Mechanical BR-32 check + manual ops flip. System displays finalSharesPaid ≥ minRoyaltyShares result on the recovery dashboard. Ops clicks one of two buttons: "Mark Successful" (releases 80%) when the check passes, or "Refund all" → Unsuccessful when it fails.
Day 50 → 60
"Up to 30 days" buffer. Communicate this to creators — actual worst case is 20 days; 10-day safety buffer.
Weeks → months
Creator delivers → ops submits Spotify URL (BR-35 Step 1) → "Release Fund" button surfaces in back office → ops clicks (BR-35 Step 2) → status = Released → 20% tranche releases. Triggers N13 release email to backers.
Post-MVP
Royalty reporting (BR-36/37/38) ships as display-only / non-functional in MVP — Pat designs the empty-state surfaces; live wiring lands next release. Fan wallet (BR-49) deferred to mid-July.

Beamco-specific lifecycle notes

DIFFERENCES VS A GENERIC CROWDFUNDING LIFECYCLE

♪ Setup

Royalty shares % + count. Publish = Now only (no scheduled publish). Engagement = creator-only Updates feed. Product URL = Spotify link.

♪ Monetization

Royalty source = Music distributor — Flow A (Beamco-as-label, manual weekly extract) OR Flow B (contributor on distributor, distributor pull). Flow choice still open — see O3.
